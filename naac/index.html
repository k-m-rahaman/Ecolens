What these figures mean

Totals come from shared EcoLens activity submitted by participating users. Material mass and CO₂e are estimates; diversion is recorded from user-confirmed sorting and estimated weights, not a verified waste audit. The directory is not a list of EcoLens partner institutions.

This is supporting sustainability information for a NAAC report. It is not a NAAC accreditation result or an official certificate. Schools can view the same environmental summary.
